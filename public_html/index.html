The lineup

Free online multiplayer games

Pick a game, invite your friends with a room code and play in real time.

Chess

2 players

The classic strategy game with full rules, a move list, game clocks, draw offers and drag-and-drop pieces. Play a friend head-to-head in your browser.

Uno

2-10 players

Match colours and numbers, stack penalties, call UNO and play to a target score. Private hands and a turn timer keep every round moving.

Poker

2-8 players

A friendly Texas Hold'em tournament with play-money chips: blinds, raises, all-ins, side pots and a showdown where the best five-card hand wins.

Okey

2-4 players

The Turkish tile game for up to four players: build runs and sets, use the okey as a wild tile and finish first, in classic Okey or the 101 variant.

Monopoly

2-6 players

Roll, buy properties, trade with friends, build houses and hotels, and bankrupt everyone else in this classic property trading game with auctions and house rules.

Wordle

1-12 players

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries. Play alone or race your friends to the same word, in English or Turkish, with a hard mode and timer.

Kingdomino

2-4 players

Build a kingdom with terrain dominoes using the real draft rules. Claim tiles in turn order, grow big properties and score every property by its size times its crowns.

Dice

2-8 players

Farkle, the push-your-luck dice game: roll six dice, set aside scoring dice and decide whether to bank your points or risk losing them all.

Rock Paper Scissors

2-12 players

Throw rock, paper or scissors against the whole room, with a proper Rock-Paper-Scissors-Shoot reveal. Play for points or knock players out in elimination mode.

Hamsterball

2-12 players

Roll your hamster ball around a tilting, shrinking plate in 3D, bump rivals off the edge and be the last one rolling over several rounds.

8 Ball Pool

2 players

Two-player 8 ball pool with realistic physics: aim, add spin, pocket your group and sink the 8 ball to win. Fouls give your opponent ball in hand.

Typing Test

1-12 players

Measure your words per minute and accuracy alone or race friends on the same text, in English or Turkish, with random words or quotes.

Go Fish

2-6 players

The classic family card game: ask other players for a rank, go fish when they have none, and collect the most books of four.

Pişti

2-4 players

The classic Turkish card game for two to four players: capture the pile with a matching card or a jack, score a pisti, and play single deals or to a target score.

Papaz Kaçtı

2-8 players

The Turkish cousin of Old Maid: discard your pairs, draw from your neighbour and make sure you are not the one left holding the Papaz.

Frequently asked questions

Is Web Board Games free?

Yes. All games are free to play, with no account or download required.

How do I play with friends?

Choose a nickname, create a room and share the 6-character room code, the page link or the QR code with your friends. When everyone has joined, the host picks a game and everybody taps Ready.

How does joining with a QR code work?

Every room shows a QR code. Point a phone camera at it and the room opens straight away; if your browser supports it you can also scan from the Scan button on the home page.

Can the host manage the room without playing?

Yes. Create the room with Host only enabled, or switch to host view in the lobby. The host then watches every game as a spectator and can pick, start, restart or end games, kick players and hand the host role to someone else.

How many people can play?

A room holds up to 12 players. Each game has its own limits, shown next to it in the lobby.

Can I change the rules of a game?

Yes. When a game is selected the host can change its settings in the lobby, such as time controls, target scores, board sizes, rounds and lives.

Which languages are supported?

The site, every game and the word games (Wordle and the typing test) work in English and Turkish. Switch the language with the language button in the menu or in the room settings.

Can I play alone?

Yes: Wordle and the typing test work with a single player. The other games need at least two players.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes, the site runs in any modern browser on desktop and mobile. Games with fast controls show on-screen controls on touch devices.

What happens if I lose my connection?

The page reconnects automatically and your seat is held for a while, so a short drop or a locked phone will not remove you from the room.
