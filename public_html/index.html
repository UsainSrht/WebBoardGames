The lineup

Free online multiplayer games

Pick a game, invite your friends with a room code and play in real time.

Chess

2 players

The classic strategy game with full rules: castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate and draws. Play a friend head-to-head in your browser.

Uno

2-10 players

Match colours and numbers, play skips, reverses, draw twos and wilds, and be the first to empty your hand. Private hands, 30-second turns.

Kingdomino

1-4 players

Build a 5x5 kingdom with domino-style terrain tiles. Claim tiles in turn order and score every property by its size times its crowns.

Dice

2-12 players

A quick luck-based party game: roll two dice each round, highest total scores. Five rounds, most points wins.

Rock Paper Scissors

2-12 players

Pick your move before the countdown ends. Every player is scored against every other player over three rounds.

Hamsterball

1-12 players

Roll your hamster ball around a tilting plate in 3D, bump rivals off the edge and be the last one standing.

Frequently asked questions

Is Web Board Games free?

Yes. All games are free to play, with no account or download required.

How do I play with friends?

Choose a nickname, create a room and share the 6-character room code, the page link or the QR code with your friends. When everyone has joined, the host picks a game and everybody taps Ready.

How does joining with a QR code work?

Every room shows a QR code. Point a phone camera at it and the room opens straight away; if your browser supports it you can also scan from the Scan button on the home page.

Can the host manage the room without playing?

Yes. Create the room with Host only enabled, or switch to host view in the lobby. The host then watches every game as a spectator and can pick, start, restart or end games, kick players and hand the host role to someone else.

How many people can play?

A room holds up to 12 players. Each game has its own limits: Chess is 2 players, Kingdomino 1-4, Uno 2-10, and Dice, Rock Paper Scissors and Hamsterball up to 12.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes, the site runs in any modern browser on desktop and mobile. Hamsterball shows an on-screen joystick on touch devices.

What happens if I lose my connection?

The page reconnects automatically and your seat is held for a while, so a short drop or a locked phone will not remove you from the room.
